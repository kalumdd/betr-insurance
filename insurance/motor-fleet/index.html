FAQs

Common questions

How many vehicles do I need for a fleet policy?

It varies by insurer. Some start at two or three vehicles. Tell us what you run and we’ll find the best fit.

Can different types of vehicle go on one policy?

Usually, yes. A mixed fleet of cars, vans and commercial vehicles can often be insured together.
