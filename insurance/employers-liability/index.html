Cover that fits the way you work

If an employee is injured or becomes ill because of their work, they may claim compensation from you. Employers’ liability insurance covers those claims and, for most UK businesses with staff, it’s a legal requirement.

Who it’s for

  • Any business that employs staff, including part-time and temporary workers
  • Businesses using labour-only sub-contractors
  • Charities and clubs with paid staff

FAQs

Common questions

Is employers’ liability insurance compulsory?

For most employers in the UK, yes. The Employers’ Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969 requires at least £5 million of cover, and you can be fined for not having it. Some businesses, such as some family businesses, may be exempt, so ask us if you’re unsure.

Do I need to display my certificate?

You must make your certificate available to employees, either displayed or electronically. We’ll send it to you as soon as cover starts.
