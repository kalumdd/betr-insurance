FAQs

Common questions

How do I work out my rebuild cost?

The rebuild cost is not the market value. It’s what it would cost to rebuild your home. We can point you to the right tools or arrange a professional valuation.

Can you insure a property that’s unoccupied or being renovated?

Yes. Specialist insurers cover unoccupied homes and renovations, which standard policies often exclude.
