Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, improve your experience, and gather information about how the site is used.

Home  ›  Cookies Policy

How We Use Cookies

At BETR Insurance Brokers, we use cookies to:

  • Ensure our website works properly
    (e.g. page navigation, secure areas, form submissions)
  • Improve your experience
    (e.g. remembering your preferences or login details)
  • Understand how our site is used
    (e.g. tracking visits, popular pages, and user journeys)
  • Support marketing and analytics
    (e.g. Google Analytics, social media integrations)

We do not use cookies to collect personal information without your consent or to pass data to third parties for advertising purposes.

Types of Cookies We Use

Type of Cookie

Purpose

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies are stored
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies

Please note: Disabling cookies may affect how our website functions and your experience.

To learn more about managing cookies, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.
